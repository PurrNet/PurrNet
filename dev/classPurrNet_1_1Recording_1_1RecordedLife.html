Detailed Description

One stream of the recording: what it is, and where its rows go in the current chunk. Per-tick streams (state, input, transform, player) get one raw row per tick while their owner exists; the encoder drops the ones that did not change.

What a recorder keeps for the streams of one object it visits every tick: the last tick it was there, and whether any of the values behind those streams changed since their rows were last written.
